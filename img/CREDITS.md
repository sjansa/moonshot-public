# Pictures

All three are NASA material, free to use (NASA media usage guidelines; no endorsement implied).

- `milkyway.jpg` — from "Deep Star Maps 2020" (the Milky Way background layer, galactic coordinates), NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio; Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC. https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851 . Scaled to 2048 x 1024, tone-mapped (gamma 2.2, floor removed).
- `moon.jpg` — the near side, rendered (orthographic, north up, zero libration) from the "CGI Moon Kit" colour map made from Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera data, NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio. https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720
- `sun.jpg` — Solar Dynamics Observatory HMI continuum image of 16 November 2026, cropped to the disc, solar north up. Courtesy of NASA/SDO and the AIA, EVE, and HMI science teams. https://sdo.gsfc.nasa.gov/
